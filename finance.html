The Broke Mode Money Checklist

10 Moves. Make Them All.

01
Open a High-Yield Savings Account Regular savings accounts pay 0.01% APY. High-yield accounts pay 4–5%. Same FDIC insurance. Move your emergency fund today. Ally, Marcus, and SoFi are the top picks.
02
Cancel Every Unused Subscription Go through your bank statement. Highlight everything that recurs monthly. Cancel anything you didn't consciously think about in the last 30 days. Average discovery: $80–$150/month in forgotten subscriptions.
03
Automate $50 to Savings Every Payday Set it and forget it. Automatic transfers on payday mean the money is gone before you can spend it. Start at $50, raise it $10 every month. You will not miss it.
04
Get Your Free Credit Report AnnualCreditReport.com — the only federally mandated free report site. Pull all three bureaus. Look for errors. Dispute anything wrong. Errors are more common than people think and can cost you real money.
05
Call Your Internet Provider and Negotiate "I'm looking at switching to [competitor]. Can you match their promotional rate?" Works 60–70% of the time. Average savings: $20–$40/month. Takes 15 minutes. Annual value: up to $480.
06
Switch to a Rewards Credit Card — Pay It Off Monthly If you carry no balance, a 2% cash-back card on $2,000/month in spending returns $480/year. Pay in full every month. The card works for you, not against you.
07
Open a Roth IRA if You Have Earned Income $7,000/year limit (2024). Tax-free growth. Tax-free withdrawal in retirement. Fidelity and Vanguard are free to open. Put it in a target-date index fund and forget it for 30 years. This is the move.
08
Track Every Dollar for 30 Days Use your bank app, a spreadsheet, or YNAB. The goal is visibility — not restriction. Most people find $200–$400/month in spending they don't remember doing. You can't fix what you can't see.
09
Build a $1,000 Emergency Fund First Before extra debt payments, before investing, before anything else. One $800 car repair without a buffer goes on a credit card at 24% APR. The emergency fund is financial insurance. Build it first.
10
Apply the 48-Hour Rule to Every Purchase Over $30 Add it to cart. Close the tab. Come back in 48 hours. Most impulse purchases evaporate. The ones that survive were worth buying. Studies show this cuts discretionary spending by 20–30%.

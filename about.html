What Is Broke Mode Life?

Broke Mode Life is a budget lifestyle platform built for people who refuse to let a tight budget shrink their world. We cover everything from eating well on $50 a week to traveling internationally without going into debt — organized across ten real-life categories so you can find what you need fast and use it immediately.

Whether you're a student, a single parent, a side-hustler, or just someone who's tired of watching money disappear — this is your resource. Every tip is real. Every product we recommend, we'd actually buy.

Our Philosophy

We Don't Do Deprivation

Living on a budget is not a punishment. It is a skill. The people who master it are not the ones grinding themselves into dust — they are the ones who have figured out that most of what costs a fortune can be had for a fraction, if you know where to look and how to think.

We don't believe in deprivation. We believe in strategy. There's a broke-mode version of almost every experience — and most of the time, it's just as good as the expensive version. The goal is not to spend less. The goal is to get more for what you spend.

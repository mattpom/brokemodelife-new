About Us

BROKE MODE LIFE

Smart solutions for every budget. Real advice for real financial situations — no trust funds required.

What We Are

Broke Mode Life is a personal finance and budget lifestyle platform built for people who are working with what they have. We cover budgeting strategies, frugal living tips, side hustle ideas, and the mindset shifts that separate people who struggle financially from those who don't — regardless of income level.

We are not here to sell you the dream. We are here to help you build one with the resources you actually have right now.

Our Philosophy

Money is a tool. Most people were never taught how to use it. Broke Mode Life exists to fix that gap — with practical, no-fluff content that meets people where they are and gives them a clear path forward.

We believe financial freedom is not a number. It is a relationship with money that gives you options. Getting there starts with the basics, and we cover every one of them.

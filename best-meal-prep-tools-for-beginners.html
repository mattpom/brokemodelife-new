What to Skip

Avoid False Economy

Skip bundles that hide the manufacturer, products selected only because they are discounted, and anything that solves a problem you do not repeatedly have. The cheapest item is not a value when it is replaced twice.

Bottom Line

Buy the option that matches the problem you actually need to solve. “Best” means the strongest overall balance here, not the right purchase for every person.

Who picks up furniture donations in Indianapolis?

Start with Habitat ReStore if you need free pickup. If your deadline is tight, check availability and get a price from another provider before you book.

Habitat ReStore · Free pickup

Greater Indy Habitat ReStore offers free pickup for accepted donations. Its coordinator asks about condition, size, and location. Tell them your ZIP code, whether stairs are involved, and the date the furniture has to be out.

Request Habitat ReStore pickup

The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana · Service fee

This Fishers nonprofit picks up gently used furniture and typically serves addresses within 25 miles of its Fishers location. It does not offer same-day pickup. Ask for the fee and confirm your items before booking.

Check The Mustard Seed pickup details

St. Vincent de Paul Indianapolis · Pickup through ReSupply

SVdP Indy lists ReSupply for pickup and a separate furniture donation dock. Confirm the pickup price with ReSupply. If you have a vehicle, check the donation dock hours before driving over.

See SVdP Indy pickup and drop-off options

What to have ready

  • Photos and rough dimensions for each piece.
  • A note about stains, damage, stairs, elevators, or tight doorways.
  • Your ZIP code and last possible pickup date.

Keep a backup plan until a provider confirms both the items and the pickup date.

One day a year, your birthday is worth real money — if you play it right. Print this, sign up in advance, and see how many stops you can hit before midnight.

How To Play

Three Rules. That's It.

01

Sign Up Early

Most loyalty programs need 1–3 weeks to load your birthday reward. Create the free accounts today — not the morning of.

02

Every Stop Counts

This isn't pass/fail. Three stops on a work day is still a win. All fifteen makes you a legend (and possibly nauseous).

03

Verify Before You Go

Brands change offers and rules without notice. Pull up the app that morning to confirm what's actually live.

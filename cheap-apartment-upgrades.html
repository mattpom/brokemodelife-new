Buying Guide

Renter Rules to Live By

No Damage

Every upgrade on this page is renter-friendly. Command strips, tension rods, peel-and-stick — no holes that cost you your deposit.

Lighting First

Lighting is the highest-ROI upgrade in any space. Fix the lighting before you spend money on anything else. Warm light alone changes everything.

Take It With You

Buy things you can take to your next place. Curtains, organizers, lights — your upgrade follows you. Avoid building into the space.

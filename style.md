# Broke Mode Life — Style Guide

Domain: brokemodelife.com. Repo: mattpom/brokemodelife-new.

## Identity

A budget lifestyle site for grinders, side-hustlers, and budget warriors. Promise: live well without spending, practical, no fluff, no sponsored content.

## Voice and Tone

Upbeat, motivational, straight-talking. Leans on real numbers and social proof, such as reader testimonials and dollar amounts saved, rather than abstract advice.

## Color Palette

Black 0a0a0a, off-black 111111, dark surface 1a1a1a, card 161616. Lime accent c8f135, lime-dim a8ce1e. Off-white text f5f5f0, muted 888, stone 555555, border 2a2a2a.

## Typography

Headlines use Bebas Neue and Barlow Condensed for bold, condensed, high-impact type. Body text uses Barlow.

## Navigation

Food, Health, Style, Travel, Finance, Free Stuff, Shop, Etsy Shop, Blog, Deeper Dive, Join Free.

## Layout Patterns

A high-contrast dark theme uses a single loud lime accent for calls to action and numbers. A "trusted resources we reference" logo strip (NerdWallet, Budget Bytes, and similar sites) builds credibility. A "pick your biggest money problem" module acts as a decision-tree instead of a plain nav. Testimonial cards show reader initials plus city and state.

## Deeper Dive

Internal page at /deeper-dive.html, embeds the shared Pinterest profile widget. Present in nav on every page with a real nav; listed in sitemap.xml.

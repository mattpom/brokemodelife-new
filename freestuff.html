Make free actually free

Keep the savings. Skip the catch.

  1. Check the total. Shipping, a required purchase, or a paid membership can turn a freebie into a bill.
  2. Know what you’re signing up for. Samples may require reviews. A free trial can renew into a paid subscription.
  3. Start local. A library card or neighborhood gift can be useful today. Sampling programs may never select you.

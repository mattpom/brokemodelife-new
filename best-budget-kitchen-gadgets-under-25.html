Buying Guide

What to Look For

Materials

Stainless steel and silicone last. Cheap plastic warps and stains. Spend an extra $3 for metal where it counts — peelers, whisks, tongs.

One Job Rule

Avoid gadgets that do one specific trick. The best budget tools do multiple jobs. A thermometer works on meat, candy, and oil.

Dishwasher Safe

If it's not dishwasher safe, you'll use it less. Friction is the enemy of cooking at home. Make it easy on yourself.
